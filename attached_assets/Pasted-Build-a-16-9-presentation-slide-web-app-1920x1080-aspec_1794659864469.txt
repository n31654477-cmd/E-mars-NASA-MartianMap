Build a 16:9 presentation slide web app (1920x1080 aspect ratio) using React, Three.js (@react-three/fiber and @react-three/drei), and Tailwind CSS for our NASA Space Apps Challenge project: "Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map" by Team E-Mars.

This must function as our high-impact Starter Slide (Slide 1) that we can both present live and export/capture cleanly for PowerPoint.

### Visual & 3D Scene Requirements:
1. **Martian Terrain:** Create a detailed 3D procedural Martian surface mesh (rust-red/terracotta tones, subtle elevation noise, craters, and rocky ridges) set against a dark space/atmospheric horizon (#0B0E14).
2. **Astronaut & Destination:**
   - Place a stylized 3D NASA astronaut (white spacesuit, gold-tinted visor, backpack PLSS unit) at the starting coordinates on the near side of the terrain, facing toward a distant elevated geological ridge (like Mount Sharp boxwork formations).
   - Place a glowing cyan target beacon marker at the destination on the distant ridge.
3. **Two Dual-Path Splines (Initially hidden in Camera View 1, fading in smoothly during the transition to View 2):**
   - **SAFEST PATH (Emerald Green `#22C55E` glowing tube/line):** Curves around craters and steep elevation gradients, staying on low-slope terrain.
   - **FASTEST PATH (Amber/Red `#F59E0B` / `#EF4444` dashed glowing tube/line):** Cuts directly in a near-straight line across steep crater rims and high-hazard terrain.
4. **Smooth Camera Transition (The Core Shot):**
   - **State 1 (Initial Over-the-Shoulder Shot):** Position the camera directly behind and slightly above the astronaut's back/shoulder, looking forward across the rocky terrain toward the distant ridge.
   - **State 2 (Isometric Tactical View):** When triggered (via a subtle "Start Mission" / "Play Transition" trigger or pressing Spacebar), smoothly interpolate (`lerp` over ~2.5 seconds) the camera upward and outward into a 45-degree **Isometric Tactical View** framing the entire route from the astronaut to the destination beacon, revealing the Safest and Fastest path lines and floating 3D waypoint tags.
